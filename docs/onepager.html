TapTen — the 10-second finger-tapping test, measured privately by any webcam

UnivaBio 2026 · AI for Human Health · individual entry · live: ryugi62.github.io/tapten · code: github.com/Ryugi62/tapten

Problem

People with Parkinson's disease see a neurologist every few months. Between visits, how their movement changed when medication wore off is reported from memory. At the bedside, clinicians rate the finger-tapping test (MDS-UPDRS item 3.4): tap index finger on thumb, fast and big, for 10 s — and watch for taps that get slower and smaller (the “decrement”). Phone screen-tap tests capture speed but cannot see size.

What TapTen does

  • Webcam test: pick the hand, tag medication state (on / wearing off / not sure), 3-2-1, tap for 10 s.
  • Numbers first: taps in 10 s, taps/s, typical opening (% of hand length), size change from the first 3 to the last 3 taps, rhythm unevenness, pauses — with a bar for every tap.
  • Quality gate: refuses to report when the hand was lost (<85 % of frames), the camera was too slow (<15 fps), the hand was too small, or the test was too short.
  • Private diary with on/off colouring and a one-page clinic sheet (print / PDF). Also analyses a video file.

How it works

Google MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (pre-trained, Apache-2.0) gives 21 3D hand points per frame inside the browser. Opening = 3D thumb-tip↔index-tip distance ÷ wrist↔middle-knuckle length (distance to camera cancels out). Gap-fill → 3-frame median → 60 ms smoothing → hysteresis peak/trough detection with a threshold of ¼ of the recording's own opening range → metrics. Pure-JS domain code, 25 unit tests, Clean Architecture layers checked in CI-style script. No model was trained.

Is it accurate? A ground-truth benchmark

We have no patient videos (that needs ethics approval). So a rigged 3D hand (MIT-licensed WebXR model) is animated with a known tap schedule — 1.5–4.5 taps/s, shrinking 0–60 %, pauses, 3 views, several distances — rendered, and run through the same tracker and code.

Privacy, enforced in code

The page's Content-Security-Policy is connect-src 'self'; model and engine are bundled; no account. While testing we found the tracking runtime tries to send a usage log to odml.pa.googleapis.com — the policy blocks it (browser console, 2026-10-06).

Who it is for

People already living with Parkinson's and their care partners, to bring an objective home record — taken at the times that matter (morning “off”, after a dose) — to appointments. Neurologists get a dated table, not a recollection.

Finished vs. planned (honest scope)

Finished: webcam test, video-file mode, quality gate, diary, clinic sheet, 36-clip benchmark, 390 px mobile layout.
Planned: validation with real patients and clinicians; both hands per session; tremor and rest measures.

Not a diagnosis, not an MDS-UPDRS score, not a medical device. Synthetic-hand accuracy is not clinical accuracy. Related research: video-based finger-tapping quantification (e.g., FastEval Parkinsonism, PMC10853559; arXiv 2308.14679) targets clinicians — TapTen is a patient-held, on-device diary.
